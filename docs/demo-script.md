# Recommended 3-Minute Hackathon Demo Script

## 0:00 - 0:30: Vision & Hero Screen
- Open `http://localhost:3000/dashboard`.
- Explain: "WATCHDOG is not a chatbot or a static dashboard. It's a persistent autonomous web intelligence agent."
- Highlight the **Attention Saved** metric (`3h 42m` human research avoided) and **Live Execution Mode** (`DEMO / LIVE`).

## 0:30 - 1:30: Hero Scenario 1 (Sony Price Drop Qualification)
- Click **"1. Price Drop"** on the Demo Control Center bar.
- Show the **Live Intelligence Feed Card**: Price dropped from ₹27,999 to ₹24,499.
- Click **"View Investigation"** to show:
  - Before / After State comparison.
  - Multi-source Evidence Graph (Amazon price + Sony distributor match + price history).
  - Rationale: "Qualifying deal confirmed. Cart prepared."

## 1:30 - 2:15: Hero Scenario 5 (Autonomous Failure Recovery)
- Click **"5. Action Recovery"** on the Demo Control Center bar.
- Show the **10-Step Execution Timeline**:
  - `ACT`: Primary Anakin Wire action failed with 503 error.
  - `LEARN / REPLAN`: WATCHDOG automatically replanned to execute via Anakin Browser workflow.
  - `VERIFY`: Action state verified clean.

## 2:15 - 3:00: Natural Language Mission Creation & Action Approval
- Click **"+ New Mission"**.
- Enter: `"Watch MacBook Air M4 and alert me if price drops below ₹85,000 from a 4.3+ rated seller."`
- Show parsed mission structure and activate.
- Navigate to **Actions** tab and click **"Approve & Execute"** to demonstrate human-in-the-loop authorization.
